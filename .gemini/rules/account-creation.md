# Rule: Account Creation Requirements

When new account users create an account in FightBracket Pro, the following requirements are strictly enforced:

1. **Usertag / Gamer Tag (Required)**:
   - Users are required and absolutely must enter their usertag (gamer tag).
   - This field is mandatory and cannot be skipped or left blank.
   - Used as the player's primary identity across brackets, leaderboards, matches, and community features.

2. **First Name (Required)**:
   - Users are required and absolutely must enter their first name.
   - Mandatory field during account creation.

3. **Last Name (Required)**:
   - Users are required and absolutely must enter their last name.
   - Mandatory field during account creation.

4. **Birthday (Optional, Year is kept private)**:
   - Entering birthday is optional for users during registration.
   - Privacy guarantee: The birth year is kept strictly private and must NEVER be exposed publicly on player profiles or to other users. Only the month and day may be shown (e.g. "April 15").

5. **Email Address (Required by Supabase)**:
   - Email is required by Supabase authentication for account identity, verification, and password recovery.
