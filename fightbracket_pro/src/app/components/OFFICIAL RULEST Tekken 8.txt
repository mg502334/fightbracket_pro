(“BNEI”) (BNEA, BNEE, and BNEI are collectively “BNE”), and is subject to these Official Rules (the “Rules”). The Tournament Group reserves the right, at its discretion, to change, modify, add, or remove portions of these Rules, provided that any changes are effective immediately and will be made available on the Tournament Website, which each Player hereby agrees to periodically visit and check for any changes, updates, or additions to these Rules. These Rules, including any additional terms or conditions incorporated by reference herein, sets forth the entire understanding and agreement between the Tournament Group and Player with respect to Player’s participation in the Tournament.

TOURNAMENT STRUCTURE. The Tournament will be divided into different Tournament Events, each with the opportunity to earn Points toward qualifying to compete in the Global Finals, as further set forth below:
Master Events. “Master+” and “Master”, collectively “Master Events”, are prestigious open offline Tournament Events, designated as Master Events by BNE, where Players from any permitted region can participate to earn large amounts of Points. Master+ or Master designation may be revoked if the tournament is unable to be held offline. 
Challenger Events. "Challenger Events" are independently operated offline Tournament Events that have been selected by the Tournament Group where Players earn points as set forth below. The Tournament Group is not involved in or responsible for Challenger Events and organizers of Challenger Events shall be solely responsible for organizing, operating, and reporting results from, each Challenger Event. Except for the Prizes provided by BNE, Challenger Events are not sponsored by, endorsed by, administered by, or associated with the Tournament Group. By entering a Challenger Event, each Player hereby releases and agrees to hold the Tournament Group harmless from any liability arising out of such Player’s participation in the applicable Challenger Event. Any questions, comments, or complaints regarding a Challenger Event should be directed to the appropriate organizer of the applicable Challenger Event and not to the Tournament Group. BNE and the Tournament Organizer reserve the right in their sole discretion to disqualify Points earned by Players in any Challenger Event if BNE or the Tournament Organizer suspects any Challenger Event of incorrectly calculating points, engaging in or allowing outcome fixing, failing to comply with any provision of these Rules, or other facts and circumstances that cause BNE or the Tournament Organizer to question the integrity of the results of such Challenger Event. 
Dojo Events. “Dojo Events”, are independently operated open offline Tournament Events that have opted in to be recognized as part of the Tournament via an application process on the Tournament Portal (as defined below), or other website designated by BNE. Dojo Events are required to use the Tournament Portal (or other websites designated by BNE) to (a) list their Dojo Event; (b) take registration; and (c) setup and record the bracket. Dojo Events are required to comply with these Rules, including the Tournament format and ruleset set forth in Section 4. The Tournament Group is not involved in or responsible for Dojo Events and organizers of Dojo Events shall be solely responsible for organizing, operating, and reporting results from, each Dojo Event. Dojo Events are not sponsored by, endorsed by, administered by, or associated with the Tournament Group. By entering a Dojo Event, each Player hereby releases and agrees to hold the Tournament Group harmless from any liability arising out of such Player’s participation in the applicable Dojo Event. Any questions, comments, or complaints regarding a Dojo Event should be directed to the appropriate organizer of the applicable Dojo Event and not to the Tournament Group. BNE and the Tournament Organizer reserve the right in their sole discretion to disqualify Points earned by Players in any Dojo Event if BNE or the Tournament Organizer suspects any Dojo Event of incorrectly calculating points, engaging in or allowing outcome fixing, failing to comply with any provision of these Rules, or other facts and circumstances that cause BNE or the Tournament Organizer to question the integrity of the results of such Dojo Event. Varying amounts of Points will be available for Players to earn at Dojo Events, based on the number of registrants. The Dojo Event submission process is as follows:
After a Dojo is created by a Tournament Event Organizer using a tournament platform supported by the Tournament Portal, Dojo Events will be submitted for review through the Tournament Portal. The Tournament Event Organizer must submit the Dojo Event through the Tournament Portal for all platforms, after which BNE and the Tournament Organizer will decide if the Dojo eligibility criteria set forth herein have been met. Tournament Event Organizers are solely responsible for ensuring their Dojo Event meets the eligibility requirements set forth herein. BNE and the Tournament Organizer reserves the right to deny Dojo Event status at their sole discretion.
 A Tournament Event Organizer is limited to hosting only one (1) Tournament Event within a ninety-six (96) hour time period. Additionally, it is prohibited for a Tournament Event Organizer to reuse a venue that hosted a Tournament Event for hosting additional Tournament Event within the same ninety-six (96) hour timeframe.
Dojo Events that meet the requirements set forth herein will be added to the tour by BNE and the Tournament Organizer. After the Dojo Event’s conclusion, Dojo Events will be reassessed based on archived stream footage before Points will be allocated based on the Point System set forth below. Dojo Events that are suspected of fraud, manipulation, attempt to mislead BNE and/or the Tournament Organizer, and or are otherwise in violation of these Rules (including Section 10), may face disciplinary action at the sole discretion of BNE and the Tournament Organizer. Falsely reporting Player disqualifications in the tournament can lead to the nullification of points and removal of future Dojo Event eligibility.
Points System. Points will be available at Master Events, Challenger Events, and Dojo Events as set forth in the chart below. Total Points earned during the Tournament will help determine the list of Players invited to compete and the bracket seeding in the Global Finals.
If two (2) or more Players in the Global Rankings have the same number of Points, the tiebreaker will be resolved in favor of a single Player by applying the following tiebreaking mechanisms, in order of application: (a) highest Points earned from an individual Master+ Event, Master Event, Challenger Event or Dojo Event; (b) highest total Points earned from all participated  Master+ Events, Master Events, Challenger Events, and Dojo Events; or (c) a custom tie-breaker solution as determined by BNE.
If two (2) or more Players in the Regional Rankings have the same number of Points, the tiebreaker will be resolved in favor of a single Player by applying the following tiebreaking mechanisms, in order of application: (a) highest Points earned from an individual Dojo Event; (b) highest Point total earned from two (2) highest individual Dojo Events; (c) highest Point total earned from three (3) highest individual Dojo Events; (d) highest Point total earned from four (4) highest individual Dojo Events; (e) highest Point total earned from five (5) highest individual Dojo Events; (f) highest total Points earned from all participated Dojo Events; (g) highest total number of Dojo Events entered; or (h) a custom tie-breaker solution as determined by BNE.

Rankings.
Global Leaderboard. Players will be ranked based on the highest total Points at Player’s top four (4) placing Master+ and Master events, top two (2) placing Challenger Events, and top two (2) placing Dojo Events. A Player is allowed to enter any number of Master Events, Challenger Events, and Dojo Events. Players can enter Master Events, Challenger Events, and Dojo Events from any region.
Regional Leaderboard. Players will be ranked based on the highest total Points at Player’s top six (6) placing Dojo Events in their selected Region. A Player is allowed to enter any number of Dojo Events. Players will only be ranked on the Regional Leaderboard for their selected region. Players must be physically located and a legal resident of a country in their selected region and may be asked to provide documentation (e.g., valid government issued ID, utility bill, etc.). Such documentation shall only be used for verification purposes.
If a Player is in a qualifying Ranking for the Global Finals on both the Global and Regional Leaderboards, the qualifying spot earned from the Global Leaderboard will take priority over the Regional Leaderboard qualification spot. The Regional Leaderboard qualification spot will then be given to the next highest Ranked Player on their Regional Leaderboard.
Tournament Portal. The Tournament Website will have a link to a separate website (available at: https://tekken-esports.bn-ent.net/) owned and operated by BNEI where players can access Ranking information and participate in Tournament Events (the “Tournament Portal”). For a Player’s Points to be counted and information to be displayed in the Rankings, a Player must: (a) register on the Tournament Portal and consent to applicable terms and conditions; and (b) have a valid account for any applicable tournament platform supported by the Tournament Portal. Accordingly, each Player acknowledges and agrees that if Player does not complete the foregoing requirements, such Player’s Points will not be counted and such Player’s Ranking information will not be displayed in the Rankings, which could result in Player’s being ineligible to receive Prizes. The Tournament Portal may be incomplete or contain errors or inaccuracies that could cause the display of inaccurate or incomplete information, failures, corruption and/or loss of data or information. Players expressly acknowledge and agree that, to the extent permitted by applicable law, all use of the Tournament Portal is at Players’ sole risk and responsibility. BNE IS PROVIDING THE Tournament Portal TO PLAYERS SOLELY ON AN “AS IS” BASIS AND WITHOUT ANY WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, WHETHER EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION, THE IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, NON-INFRINGEMENT, ACCURACY, COMPLETENESS, PERFORMANCE, AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.
Live Streaming. Each Tournament Event Organizer will provide BNE and Tournament Organizer a link to their live streaming channel with recorded archives or Video(s) on Demand ("VODs") with a permanent hosting link that does not expire upon registering their event on the Tournament Portal, and must stream at a minimum the Top 8 of their event so that BNE and Tournament Organizer can review the stream for event information and compliance with these Rules. Tournament Organizers must follow any additional requirements listed in the Tournament Organizer Guide available on the Tournament Portal. 
Global Finals.  
Global Finals Qualification.
The Global Finals will feature thirty-six (36) Players qualified through the following pathways:
Four (4) Players ranked 1st through 4th on the Global Leaderboard Rankings qualified directly for the Final Bracket.
Sixteen (16) Players qualified for the Global Leaderboard Group Stage, consisting of:
Players ranked 5th through 16th on the Global Leaderboard Rankings.
The winner of each Master+ Event.
The winner of the TEKKEN 8 Esports World Cup 2026 competition.
Fifteen (15) Players qualified through the Regional Leaderboard Rankings, consisting of the 1st Place Player from each Regional Leaderboard.
One (1) Player qualified for the Final Bracket through the Last Chance Qualifier (LCQ).
If a Player qualifies for the Global Finals through multiple qualification pathways, only one (1) qualification slot shall be retained by that Player. Qualification priority and slot reallocation shall be determined as follows:
If a Player qualifies through the Global Leaderboard, a Master+ Event or the Tekken 8 Esports World Cup 2026 and the Regional Leaderboard Rankings, the qualification slot earned through the Global Leaderboard Rankings or a Master+ Event or the Tekken 8 Esports World Cup 2026 shall take priority. The Regional Leaderboard qualification slot shall then be awarded to the next highest Ranked Player on the applicable Regional Leaderboard.
If a qualification slot obtained through a Master+ Event and/or the Tekken 8 Esports World Cup 2026 becomes vacant due to a Player already qualifying for the Global Finals through another qualification pathway, the vacant slot shall be reallocated to the next highest Ranked Player on the Global Leaderboard.
Global Leaderboard Group Stage.
Participants. The only Players eligible to participate in the Global Leaderboard Group Stage Pools are the following:
Rankings. Players ranked 5th through 16th on the Global Leaderboard Rankings.
The winner of each Master+ Event and the winner of the Tekken 8 Esports World Cup 2026.
Global Leaderboard Group Stage Format.
The sixteen (16) qualified Players will compete in four (4) round robin groups of four (4) Players.
Each Group will play a series of round-robin best-of-three Matches, in which the Player who wins two (2) Sets first wins the Match.
The top two (2) placings from each Group will advance to the Final Bracket phase of the tournament.
The Players advancing to the Final Bracket from each Group will be determined by the following, in order of priority:
First: Number of Match wins.
Second: The net Set win score (Total Set wins minus Set losses).
Third: The head-to-head Match results between the tied Players.
In the event of a three (3) Player tie, a best-of-one round robin will take place between these three (3) Players, with the first Player to win two (2) Sets winning the tie. In the event of a three (3) Player tie where the top two (2) Players need to be determined in order to advance to the Final Bracket, then the first Player to lose two (2) Sets will be eliminated.
Global Leaderboard Group Stage Seeding.
The selection order will be determined as follows:
The sixteen (16) Players qualified for the Global Group Stage will be seeded according to their final position on the Tournament's Global Leaderboard Rankings.
If two (2) or more Players in the Global Leaderboard Rankings have the same number of Points, the tiebreaker will be resolved in favor of a single Player by applying the following tiebreaking mechanisms, in order of application: (a) highest Points earned from an individual Master+ Event, Master Event, Challenger Event or Dojo Event; (b) highest total Points earned from all participated Master+ Events, Master Events, Challenger Events, and Dojo Events; or (c) a custom tie-breaker solution as determined by BNE.
Players will select their Group Stage placement in the following order:
Players seeded 13th through 16th will select first, in the following order:16th, 15th, 14th, 13th.
Players seeded 9th through 12th will then select, in the following order: 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th.
Players seeded 5th through 8th will then select in the following order: 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th.
Players seeded 1st through 4th will select last, in the following order: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th.
Players seeded 1st through 4th can interrupt the Group selection process between the 16th to 5th selections to select a Group.
If two (2) or more Players seeded 1st through 4th want to interrupt the Group selection process at the same time, the Player with the highest seed decides which Player interrupting the selection process will make a Group selection.
Players may only select from the remaining available Group Stage positions at the time of their selection.
Regional Leaderboard Group Stage.
Participants. The only Players eligible to participate in the Regional Leaderboard Group Stage are the following:
 Rankings. Top 1 Player from each of the fifteen (15) Regional Leaderboard Rankings.
Regional Leaderboard Group Stage Format.
The fifteen (15) qualified Players will compete in three (3) round robin groups of five (5) Players.
Qualified Players will be placed into one (1) of the three (3) round robin groups of five (5) Players drawn at random.
Each Group will play a series of round-robin best-of-three Matches, in which the Player who wins two (2) Sets first wins the Match.
The top one (1) placings from each Group will advance to the Final Bracket phase of the tournament.
The Players advancing to the Final Bracket from each Group will be determined by the following, in order of priority:
First: Number of Match wins.
Second: The net Set win score (Total Set wins minus Set losses).
Third: The head-to-head Match results between the tied Players.
In the event of a three (3) Player tie, the tied Players shall compete in a best-of-one round robin tiebreaker. The Player advancing from the Group shall be determined by applying the tiebreaker criteria set forth above to the results of the tiebreaker matches.
Regional Leaderboard Group Stage Seeding.
Qualified Players will be placed into one (1) of the three (3) round robin groups of five (5) Players drawn at random.
Last Chance Qualifier (LCQ).
Participants & Seeding.
The LCQ will consist of Players who are eligible to compete from any of the eligible Territories of the Tournament except for the Players already advancing to the Final Bracket.
Players that did not advance to the Final Bracket from the Global Leaderboard Group Stage and the Regional Leaderboard Group Stage are eligible to participate in the LCQ.
Players that did not participate in the Global Leaderboard or Regional Leaderboard Group Stages will be seeded by their highest total Points allocation from the Global Leaderboard Rankings.
Players that participated in the Global Leaderboard or Regional Leaderboard Group Stages will benefit from pre-established seeds decided by the Tournament Group.
The order in which Players will choose a spot will be determined in the following order:
Global Leaderboard Group Stage 3rd Place
The Player with the highest Global Leaderboard Rank will have priority in choosing a spot.
Global Leaderboard Group Stage 4th Place
The Player with the highest Global Leaderboard Rank will have priority in choosing a spot.
Regional Leaderboard Group Stage 2nd Place
2nd Place Players will participate in a random draw to determine the order of choosing a spot in the LCQ.
Regional Leaderboard Group Stage 3rd Place
3rd Place Players will participate in a random draw to determine the order of choosing a spot in the LCQ.
Regional Leaderboard Group Stage 4th Place
4th Place Players will participate in a random draw to determine the order of choosing a spot in the LCQ.
Regional Leaderboard Group Stage 5th Place
5th Place Players will participate in a random draw to determine the order of choosing a spot in the LCQ.
LCQ Format.
The LCQ format will follow that of the Tournament Format & Rules as set forth in Section 4.
Final Bracket.
The Top Sixteen (16) Final Bracket will be conducted as a Double Elimination Bracket Competition with the:
Four (4) Players ranked 1 to 4 from the Global Leaderboard.
Eight (8) Players that advanced from the Global Leaderboard Group Stage.
Three (3) Players that advanced from the Regional Leaderboard Group Stage.
One (1) Player that placed 1st in the Last Chance Qualifier (LCQ).
Final Bracket Seeding.
Round 1 Winners Side Bracket.
Match 1: Global Leaderboard TOP 1 vs. Regional Group C 1st Place
Match 2: Global Group D 1st Place vs Global Group A 2nd Place.
Match 3: Global Leaderboard TOP 4 vs. LCQ 1st Place
Match 4: Global Group C 1st Place vs Global Group B 2nd Place
Match 5: Global Leaderboard TOP 2 vs Regional Group B 1st Place
Match 6: Global Group A 1st Place vs. Global Group D 2nd Place
Match 7: Global Leaderboard TOP 3 vs Regional Group A 1st Place
Match 8: Global Group B 1st Place vs Global Group C 2nd Place
Top 16 (sixteen) Matches.
Round 1 Winners Side Bracket, Winner’s Quarter Finals, Losers Round 1 and Losers Round 2 will be best-of-three in which the Player who wins two (2) Sets first wins the Match.
Top 8 (eight) Matches.
Winner's Semi Finals, Losers Top 8 (eight), Losers Quarter Finals, Losers Semi Finals, Winners Finals, Losers Finals, and Grand Finals Matches will be best-of-five in which the Player who wins three (3) Sets first wins the Match.
TOURNAMENT FORMAT & RULES.
Double Elimination. Each Tournament Event will be played as a double elimination (a “Double Elimination”) bracket competition, in which Players compete in Matches starting from what is known as the winner’s side of the bracket (the “Winner’s Bracket”). If a Player loses a Match while in the Winner's Bracket, that Player will be moved to the loser’s side of the bracket (the “Loser’s Bracket”). If a Player loses a Match while in the Loser’s Bracket, that Player is eliminated and will no longer advance further in the bracket for that Tournament Event. The winner of the Winner’s Bracket will play against the winner of the Losers Bracket in the Grand Finals of the Tournament Event, as set forth below.
Match Play.
Master+ Events:
All Matches will be best-of-three, in which the Player who wins two (2) Sets first wins the Match, with the exception that the Winner’s Bracket Semi-Finals, Losers Bracket Round 1, Losers Bracket Quarter-Finals, Losers Bracket Semi-Finals, Winners Bracket Finals and Grand Finals in the Top 8 of each Tournament Event bracket will be best-of-five, in which the Player who wins three (3) Sets first wins the Match.
Master Events:
All Matches will be best-of-three, in which the Player who wins two (2) Sets first wins the Match, with the exception that the Winner’s Bracket Semi-Finals, Losers Bracket Round 1, Losers Bracket Quarter-Finals, Losers Bracket Semi-Finals, Winners Bracket Finals and Grand Finals in the Top 8 of each Tournament Event bracket will be best-of-five, in which the Player who wins three (3) Sets first wins the Match.
Challenger and Dojo Events:
All Matches will be best-of-three, in which the Player who wins two (2) Sets first wins the Match, with the exception that the Winner’s Bracket finals, Losers Bracket semi-finals, Losers Bracket finals, and Grand Finals of each Tournament Event bracket will be best-of-five, in which the Player who wins three (3) Sets first wins the Match.
Tournament Event Organizers for Challenger and Dojo Events may instead opt for the exception that the Winner’s Bracket Semi-Finals, Losers Bracket Round 1, Losers Bracket Quarter-Finals, Losers Bracket Semi-Finals, Winners Bracket Finals and Grand Finals in the Top 8 of each Tournament Event bracket will be best-of-five, in which the Player who wins three (3) Sets first wins the Match.
Tournament Event Organizers must make the choice of Top 4 best-of-five or Top 8 best-of-five before their tournament begins.
The winner of any single Set is determined when the Game awards the win to that Player or upon the opponent causing a match disruption or other violation of the Rules as determined by the Tournament Organizer staff member.
During a Match, the winner of any single Set must comply with the following winner lock rules: 
At the start of a Match:
Players select their sides. Players may mutually agree on who sits on left and right side, corresponding to Player 1 and Player 2, respectively, at the start of a Set. Players are committed to this selection for the entirety of the Match. If Players cannot agree to a Side Selection, they will play a Best-of-Three Rock-Paper-Scissors to determine who picks their side first. The winner of the Best-of-Three Rock-Paper-Scissors picks their side first and will be committed to that selection for the entirety of the Match.
Each Player selects their character from all selectable characters and the stage is selected at random;
A Match begins when Players enter the "Get ready for the next battle!" VS screen after Stage selection;
After any single Set, the winner is "locked" into their character and they are not permitted to switch their character until or unless they lose a subsequent single Set;
All single Sets are loser's choice; and
The loser of any single Set may choose between returning to stage select and choosing a new randomly selected stage or returning to character select to choose a new character and a new randomly selected stage.
All Matches must use the following in-Game Settings:
Tournament Settings: Default
The tournament console used for streaming must use Preset 1
Screen Settings
In-Battle Status Settings: On
Versus ("VS") Mode
Rounds: 3 out of 5
Time Limit: 60 seconds
Stage: Random
Special Style is allowed during Match Play
Character Customizations: Off
All Character Preset Costumes are legal for Tournament use, unless BNE determines, in its sole discretion, that certain costumes give an unfair advantage. 
Players cannot use the same Preset. Should both Players wish to use the same Preset, they will play a Best-Of-One Rock-Paper-Scissors tiebreaker to determine who may use their preferred Preset Costume for the duration of the Match.
If a Character Customization or one of the above listed Presets that cannot be used are used, the Set is subject to not being scored. Players are responsible for immediately finishing the Set with one (1) Player holding forward for the remainder of the rounds to quickly return to the lobby and restart the Set.
Players must use a Game version on one of the following platforms as determined by the Tournament Organizer or as otherwise set forth herein:
PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, or Steam.
Player Options. Prior to commencement of a Match, Players may exercise the following options:
Blind Pick. Players may request a method of character selection known as blind pick (“Blind Pick”) to keep their character selection secret until the first Set begins. In the Blind Pick process, the Players will each tell a Tournament Organizer staff member, in secret, their character selection for the first Set. Players will then select their character with the Tournament Organizer staff member validating their selections.  If a Player purposely does not select the character they told the Tournament Organizer staff member, that Player will forfeit that single Set.
Rule Violations. Players must bring up any rule violations or disruptions prior to starting or restarting a Set. If any rule violation or disruptions occur during a Set, Players must immediately notify the Tournament Organizer. If Players complete a Set without raising a rule violation or disruption, the Set will count.
Draws. In the event of a tied Set declared by a Double K.O. or timeout on the Game screen, the Set will not be scored and both Players will replay the tied Set with the same character selections.
Grand Finals. The “Grand Finals” is a Match series comprised of up to two (2) best-of-five Matches between the final two (2) Players left in a Tournament Event. In the event the Player from the Winner’s Bracket wins the first Match, the Grand Finals immediately ends, giving the Player from the Loser’s Bracket their second and eliminating loss, and making the Player from the Winner’s Bracket the overall winner of the Tournament Event. In the event that the Player from the Loser’s Bracket wins the first Match, thereby giving the Player from the Winner’s Bracket their first loss, a second best-of-five Match will be played. The Players may choose different characters and exercise any pre-Match options for this new Match. The Player that wins this final Match, thereby giving the other Player their second and eliminating loss from the Tournament Event, is declared the winner of such Tournament Event. For the sake of clarity, the foregoing shall not apply to Global Finals, the details of which are specified in Section 3.
Missing Players. Players must be present at the call time for a Match. Players that are not present and ready to play within ten (10) minutes of this call time are subject to Match forfeiture.
Match Disruptions. If a Player intentionally or accidentally causes a pause in a Match, whether by pressing the Options button or by experiencing a controller disconnection, the Player that caused the pause will forfeit the current round of the Set. If a Match disruption occurs that is out of control of the Players of the Match, such as action of another Player’s un-desynced controller or a Game software crash or console hardware failure making Players unable to continue a Set, the Tournament Organizer staff member shall order the Players to restart the Set, but with the same round count from the point that the Match disruption occurred. Players are responsible for starting a new Set and holding forward for the necessary number of rounds to quickly return to the round count that was achieved before a Match disruption.
Inevitable Defeat. If a Match Disruption occurs during a move or animation, and after the game has been unpaused, the round ends with no further inputs from either Player, a Player that caused a Match Disruption will not forfeit the current round of the Set.
Coaching. Each Tournament Organizer may provide their own set of rules for coaching. If none are provided, the following set of rules apply:
Players may designate one (1) individual to be their coach during a Match.
Coaching will only be allowed for the Player that lost the previous Set.
Players may consult with the coach only one (1) time for the Match.
Players may consult with the coach for a maximum of one (1) minute.
Coaching while a Set is in play is not allowed and is subject to Set forfeiture.
Personal electronic devices such as cellular phones, computers, tablets, etc. during a Match is not allowed.
Reading written notes will only be allowed for the Player that lost the previous Set.
Players may read written notes only (1) time for the Match.
Players may read written notes for a maximum of one (1) minute.
If a rule violation occurs, a Player can notify a Tournament Organizer.
Tournament Organizers may issue a warning for any rule violations.
Failure to adhere to coaching rules after a warning from the Judge will result in forfeiting the next Set.
Controller Usage. A Player may not program functions on a controller using methods outside the basic functions of the Game, including, but not limited to, programmable and turbo controllers. The following are sequences are prohibited:
A single input that results in the execution of multiple subsequent inputs.
Combination inputs except for those that can be applied within the game’s controller settings.
